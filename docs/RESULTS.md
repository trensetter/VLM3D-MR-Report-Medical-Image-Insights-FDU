## Local Evaluation

No local test-set metric is presented as an official score. Local results are omitted from this repository unless they can be tied to a named checkpoint, exact image and weight artifacts, input split/sample count, decoding configuration, image-selection policy, and evaluator version. Any future local results must be labeled **local** and must not include patient identifiers, reports, or per-case outputs.

The exact known platform status and artifact hashes are listed in [`SUBMISSION_ARTIFACTS.md`](SUBMISSION_ARTIFACTS.md).
